## Visual System

### Imagery Style
- **Hero photography:** High-end travel destinations — overwater bungalows, mountain vistas, city skylines at golden hour
- **Color treatment:** Photos desaturated slightly, with primary (navy) or secondary (sky blue) tint overlays at 10-15% opacity
- **People:** Diverse travelers — couples, families, solo adventurers — candid, laughing, exploring
- **Composition:** Rule of thirds, ample negative space for text overlays, golden ratio guides

### Graphic Elements
- **Line icons:** Continuous-line style, 2px stroke, rounded caps, in navy or sky blue
- **Divider lines:** Diagonal or horizontal, 1px, in accent gold (#FFD32A)
- **Badges:** Pill-shaped, with icon + text, filled with secondary color, text in white or dark

### Background Patterns
- Subtle diagonal lines at 3% opacity in navy over white
- Gradient meshes: navy → sky blue → white, vertical or 45° diagonal
- Subtle texture: paper grain at 1% opacity for print materials

## Brand Guidelines Summary

1. **Always use primary navy (#283B90) as dominant color** in logos and main headings.
2. **Secondary sky blue (#53C9E0)** for accents, highlights, secondary text, dividers.
3. **Gold accent (#FFD32A)** only for CTAs, important badges, celebratory elements — max 5% of design.
4. **Montserrat** for all headlines, titles, logos. **Figtree** for body copy, captions.
5. **Imagery** must feature real travel destinations or curated experiences — no generic “beach stock photo” clichés without purpose.
6. **Always include whitespace** — don’t cram text or graphics. Let the design breathe.
7. **CTA buttons** must use navy background with white text, or white background with navy text — never reverse on navy without a minimum 44px hit area.
8. **Dark mode**: Invert primary to sky blue, secondary to navy, accent stays gold. Text dark on light.
