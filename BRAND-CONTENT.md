## Engagement Guidelines

### Respond Within
- **Comments:** Within 2 hours during business hours (9am-6pm, Mon-Fri)
- **DMs:** Within 4 hours during business hours
- **Mentions:** Like and reply within 24 hours

### Engagement Actions
1. **Like** every comment within 1 hour
2. **Reply** to questions with helpful information
3. **Share** user-generated content (with permission, always credit)
4. **Pin** top engagement posts to profile top

### Avoid
- Buying followers or engagement
- Overly promotional "hard sell" in every post
- Ignoring negative feedback (address publicly and helpfully)
> **Golden Rule:** Always add value. Every interaction should make the follower feel heard or informed.

## Visual Content Specs

### Instagram Feed Post (1080×1080px)
- **Aspect ratio:** 1:1
- **File:** PNG (graphics) or JPG (photos, max 2MB)
- **Text:** Max 20% of image (Instagram algorithm friendly)
- **Brand colors:** Navy primary, sky blue secondary, gold accent max 5%
- **Font:** Montserrat for headlines, Figtree for body

### Instagram Story/Reel (1080×1920px)
- **Aspect ratio:** 9:16 vertical
- **Safe zone:** Keep critical content within central 80% (avoid top/bottom notches)
- **Duration:** 20-25 seconds per reel
- **Music:** Licensed tracks only; 1-2 songs max per reel
- **Captions:** Always include closed captions (30% watch without sound)

### Facebook Link Post (1200×630px)
- **Aspect ratio:** 1.91:1 link preview
- **File:** JPG or PNG
- **Title:** Max 25 characters for full display
- **Description:** Max 2 sentences (Facebook truncates after ~150 chars)
- **CTA button:** Use navy "Learn More" or "Book Now" style

### Facebook Cover (820×312px)
- **Aspect ratio:** 2.63:1
- **Primary visual:** Navy gradient + logo + one key message
- **Secondary text:** Small, readable at small sizes
- **Update:** Quarterly or with major campaigns

## Analytics Tracking

### Weekly Metrics (track every Monday)
- Reach & impressions
- Engagement rate (likes + comments + shares / reach)
- Link clicks (CTR)
- New followers
- Top-performing post (type, theme, format)

### Monthly Reports
- Follower growth (+/-)
- Reach trends (week-over-week)
- Top 3 content themes by engagement
- Hashtag performance (which drive most reach)
- Conversion rate (link clicks → bookings, if tracked)

### Key Success Indicators (quarterly)
- **Reach growth:** +15% quarter-over-quarter
- **Engagement rate:** >3% average across posts
- **Click-through rate:** >2% on link posts
- ** follower quality:** >20% from target demographics (25-45, travel-interested)
