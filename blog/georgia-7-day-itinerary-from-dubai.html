Georgia in 7 Days: Perfect One-Week Itinerary from Dubai

Home › Journal › Holidays

Seven days is the sweet spot for Georgia from Dubai — enough for icons plus one slow day. This is the exact rhythm our specialists use, built around Tbilisi, Kazbegi, Batumi.

  • Shoulder months win on price and light

Day 1 — Land and orient

Morning arrival, hotel check-in, evening walk around the base neighbourhood and an early night. Jet lag is real even on short hops — don't stack icons on day one.

Days 2–3 — The icons

Tbilisi's headline sights with early starts: the big-ticket viewpoints, the old quarter, the museum that matters. One guided day here pays for itself.

Day 4 — Culture deep-cut

Craft quarter, food market, a hands-on session — the day travellers remember. A Kazbegi extension works beautifully here.

Day 5 — Nature escape

Leave the city: coast, highlands or lake day depending on season. Pack layers — Georgia's microclimates surprise.

Day 6 — Food and free time

No alarms. Brunch, shopping street, the sight you missed, farewell dinner at the place your guide actually eats.

Day 7 — Slow finale

Late checkout where possible, last souvenirs, transfer with a 3-hour buffer. Land in Dubai with stories, not exhaustion.

If you have 10 days instead

Add a second base (usually Kazbegi) plus one true rest day mid-trip. The extra days cost less per day than the first seven — flights are already sunk.

Quick answers

Can this stretch to 10 days?

Easily — add a second base (usually Kazbegi) and a rest day mid-trip.

Is 5 days enough for Georgia?

For one city plus a day trip, yes — ask us for the compressed version.
